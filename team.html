The team

Two people. One small idea.

FitPulse was designed and built by two people who wanted a fitness reminder that gets out of the way and does its one job well.

Co-creator · Design & front-end

Creator One

Visual design, layout systems, and the front-end experience.

Responsible for the look and feel of FitPulse — the dark sunset palette, the variation cards, and everything users touch. Believes a reminder app should feel calm and confident, not noisy.

Co-creator · Engineering

Creator Two

API integration, scheduling, and the PDF pipeline.

Built the exercise API layer, the wger image and instruction pipeline, the offline fallback, and the Apps Script backend that generates the styled PDFs and emails them out. Obsessed with making sure the app never appears broken.

How we split the work

Nothing was handed off. We sat in the same room (and on the same calls), argued about button sizes, rewrote the PDF template twice, and shipped the whole thing together. Every screen you see here has both of our fingerprints on it.
