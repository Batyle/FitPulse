Why we built this

The reminder you actually listen to.

FitPulse was built for one simple reason: most people don't skip workouts because they don't know what to do — they skip because nothing told them to start.

What FitPulse does

FitPulse is a personal workout reminder tool. You tell it what kind of workout you want to do and when you want to be reminded. At the exact date and time you chose, an email arrives in your inbox with a matching exercise plan — complete with reference photos and step-by-step instructions — so the next step is always obvious.

Why we built it

Fitness apps are crowded with features. Tracking, scoring, gamification, leaderboards. But the single hardest part of any training routine isn't the workout — it's showing up. We stripped everything back to that one problem.

FitPulse doesn't ask you to log in every day. It doesn't nag. It just does one thing well: it reminds you to train at the moment you said you wanted to train.

What you get

  • Eight workout variations — yoga, HIIT, strength, cardio, mobility, pilates, spin, dance — plus any custom variation you invent.
  • Real exercise photos and instructions pulled from the open wger fitness database, matched by exercise name so you see the right move and know how to do it.
  • Instant PDF reminders that land at your chosen time with a recommended plan for the day.
  • Repeat schedules — once, daily, weekdays, or weekly.
  • A daily motivational quote — included in your PDF.
  • Zero sign-up, zero tracking, zero spam.

Who it's for

🎓

Students & beginners

You want to start moving but don't know where to begin. Pick a variation, get a plan in your inbox, follow it.

💼

Busy professionals

You have 30 minutes between meetings and need a nudge. Set a recurring weekday reminder.

🏋️

Home athletes

You train in your living room. You want variety, a clear plan, and someone telling you to start.

What FitPulse is not

It's not a trainer. It's not a coach. It's not a medical app. It won't tell you what's safe for your body — that's on you and, when needed, a professional. What it will do is make sure the workout you already planned to do doesn't slip through the cracks.
